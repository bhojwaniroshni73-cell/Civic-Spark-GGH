# SheGuard AI — 2-Minute Hackathon Demo Script

> **Team:** Civic Spark · **College:** SSIPMT Raipur/Bhilai  
> **Challenge Track:** Society & Well-being | Google Gemini Hackathon 2026  
> **Pitch Line:** *"Others call for help. SheGuard AI makes sure help understands her instantly."*  
> **Demo Duration:** 2 Minutes (Strict)

---

## ⏱️ Scene-by-Scene Presentation Flow

### [0:00 - 0:20] Hook & Sign In
- **Action:** Open [`http://localhost:3000`](http://localhost:3000). Click **"Demo Account Auto-Fill"** → Click **"Enter SheGuard AI"**.
- **Speaker:**
  > *"Respected Judges, abuse and harassment happen where a woman cannot safely speak, type, or explain herself. SheGuard AI stays with her from the first second of panic to the last page of paperwork."*

---

### [0:20 - 0:45] Feature 1: Emergency Buzzer & Live Responder Console
- **Action:**
  1. On the home dashboard, tap the large **Emergency Buzzer**. (Loud siren sounds and red strobe deterrent activates).
  2. Show the **Delivery Status List**: *"Delivered to Mom (+91 98765 00001) & Police Responder Console"*.
  3. Open the **Responder Console** tab ([`http://localhost:3000/responder.html`](http://localhost:3000/responder.html)).
  4. Show the incoming live alert card with victim details, live GPS coordinates, and click **"Dispatch Patrol"**.
  5. Tap the buzzer again on the main app to stop the siren, and show the public live tracking link ([`http://localhost:3000/tracking.html`](http://localhost:3000/tracking.html)) that her mother can open without installing any app.
- **Speaker:**
  > *"In public danger, one tap triggers our high-decibel deterrent while simultaneously broadcasting high-accuracy GPS telemetry to her emergency contacts and our live Police Responder Console with an instant PCR patrol dispatch."*

---

### [0:45 - 1:05] Feature 2: Silent Panic SOS from Disguised Calculator
- **Action:**
  1. Click **"Hide"** on top right. Show the functioning dark calculator (`15 * 6 = 90`).
  2. Type **`1234=`** to secretly unlock the **Stealth Safety Console**.
  3. Tap **"TRANSMIT SILENT SOS BEACON"**. Show the tiny neutral confirmation dot that quietly vanishes in 3 seconds with zero sound or flash.
  4. Press the **`ESC`** key to show how immediately it locks back to the innocent calculator screen!
- **Speaker:**
  > *"For women trapped in domestic abuse or stalked where speaking puts them in grave danger, SheGuard disguises as a calculator. Typing 1234= secretly unlocks her private escape plan and silent panic SOS with zero noise or screen flicker."*

---

### [1:05 - 1:25] Feature 3: Hindi Voice Triage & Multimodal AI
- **Action:**
  1. Return to Home → Click **"I need help now"**.
  2. Click the demo chip **"Hindi Road Accident"** (or click Record Voice).
  3. Click **"Get Instant Guidance & Dispatch Brief"**.
  4. Show Gemini classifying it as **CRITICAL Medical Trauma**, giving immediate pressure/CPR steps, and generating the **112 Dispatch Brief**. Click **"Read Aloud"** to let the audio speak.
- **Speaker:**
  > *"In an emergency, she doesn't have to explain from scratch in English. Gemini understands spoken Hindi, gives immediate life-saving bleeding control steps, and auto-writes a ready brief formatted for 112 responders."*

---

### [1:25 - 1:40] Feature 4: Online Trolling & Cyber Harassment Help
- **Action:**
  1. Go Home → Click **"Online Trolling & Cyber Harassment"**.
  2. Select **Instagram** → Click **"Generate Action Plan & Cyber Help"**.
  3. Show the verified legal action plan citing **cybercrime.gov.in (Helpline 1930)**, **StopNCII.org** for non-consensual image removal, NCW helpline **7827170170**, and verified IT Act Section 66E/67.
- **Speaker:**
  > *"Online violence is real violence. SheGuard guides victims of sextortion and cyberbullying with real, verified tools—linking directly to StopNCII.org and the National Cyber Crime Portal 1930."*

---

### [1:40 - 1:50] Feature 5: Sakhi — Rebranded Empathetic AI Companion
- **Action:**
  1. Go Home → Click **"Sakhi, your AI companion"**.
  2. Save a mood check-in (Safe & Calm, 7/10).
  3. Type to Sakhi: *"I feel scared walking alone"*. Notice the debounced **"Did you mean: ...?"** autocorrect chip.
  4. Show Sakhi's warm, validating response. Mention the built-in safety trigger that surfaces **Tele-MANAS (14416)** if self-harm or distress is expressed.
- **Speaker:**
  > *"Safety isn't just an SOS; it starts before danger escalates. Sakhi is her daily AI companion, offering emotional check-ins, grounding exercises, and 24x7 safety reassurance."*

---

### [1:50 - 2:00] Feature 6: Tamper-Evident Vault & BNS 2023 FIR Export
- **Action:**
  1. Go to **"Draft Police Complaint / FIR"**.
  2. Show the formal complaint citing **Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita (BNS) 2023 Sections 78 & 74** with embedded SHA-256 evidence manifest hashes.
  3. Click **"Download PDF"** to demonstrate instant client-side PDF export!
- **Speaker:**
  > *"Finally, our vault anchors multi-file evidence with browser-computed SHA-256 hashes under Section 63 of the Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam 2023, and auto-drafts a formal FIR ready to file. Others call for help. SheGuard AI makes sure help understands her instantly. Thank you!"*
